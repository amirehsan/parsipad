ParsiPad is a powerful AI-powered translation extension that makes Persian-English translation seamless and intelligent.

Grammar Learning (designed for Persian speakers studying English)
Master English grammar while you translate:
- Inline grammar notes right inside the floating translation popup on any webpage - click "Explain grammar" to see concise grammar points
- Dedicated grammar learning page with detailed explanations, examples, and an interactive quiz
- All grammar content (explanations, examples, quiz questions, quiz options) is in English so you study the language you're learning
- Quiz wrong answers reflect mistakes Persian-native English learners actually make (L1 interference: wrong word order, missing articles, wrong preposition, calque)
- Text-to-Speech for English pronunciation (the Listen button appears on the English side; Persian playback is not provided since browser Persian voices are inconsistent)
- Register badges show formal vs informal English usage
- Compare and Contrast sections explain how English and Persian handle the same grammar differently
- Common mistakes section to avoid typical errors
- Save grammar lessons to Favorites for review

Choose Your AI
Pick your preferred AI provider:
- Claude (Anthropic): Excellent for nuanced translations
- Gemini (Google): Fast and efficient
- ChatGPT (OpenAI): Versatile and reliable
Switch providers anytime in Settings.

Instant Translation
Select any text on any webpage and get accurate Persian to English or English to Persian translations instantly. ParsiPad automatically detects the language and translates accordingly.

Smart Text Polishing
Not just translation. Polish your text in three styles:
- Professional: formal, business-ready text
- Conversational: natural, friendly tone
- Concise: shorter, to-the-point version
- Regenerate any variant for fresh alternatives

Dictionary Lookup
Look up any word to get comprehensive lexical information:
- Definitions with example sentences
- Phonetic transcription (IPA)
- Synonyms and antonyms
- Persian/English translation
- Right-click or press Alt+D to look up instantly

Screenshot Region Translate
Translate text from any area on a webpage:
- Press Alt+S or right-click "Screenshot and Translate"
- Drag to select region with crosshair cursor
- AI extracts and translates text from the captured area
- Perfect for images, banners, and non-selectable text

Image Translation
Extract and translate text from images:
- Upload images or paste from clipboard
- OCR extracts text automatically
- Supports JPG, PNG, WebP (up to 5MB)

Document Translation
Translate entire documents with ease:
- Upload TXT files directly
- Progress tracking with chunk indicator
- Cancel anytime during long translations
- Download translated file when complete

Full Page Translation
Translate entire webpages instantly:
- Right-click "Translate this page" or press Alt+P
- Smart chunking for reliable translation
- Progress overlay with real-time status
- Toggle button to switch between original and translated
- Vazirmatn font for beautiful Persian text
- Cancel anytime

Analytics Dashboard
Track your API usage and estimated costs:
- Cost estimation per provider (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT)
- Action breakdown: translations, polishes, dictionary, documents, images, grammar
- Usage trends over 7 days, 30 days, or all time
- Export analytics data as JSON

Multiple Ways to Use
